Privacy Policy for Sinema

Effective 2 August 2026 · Last updated 2 August 2026

The short version

Sinema does not ask who you are and does not sell personal information. There is no account, no sign-in and no advertising. Your favourites, your theme choice and the cached film data that lets it work offline are stored only on your own device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

The app does send anonymous usage and crash data to Google Firebase — which screens are opened and what happened before a crash — so that faults can be found and fixed. It is not linked to a name or an account, because the app never learns one. Section 4 sets out exactly what is sent.

The one thing you should know: to show you films, the app requests data from The Movie Database (TMDB). Like any internet request, those requests reveal your device's IP address to TMDB. Sinema sends them nothing else about you.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy describes how the Sinema Android application ("the app") handles information. It applies to the app only. It does not apply to The Movie Database or to Google Play, each of which has its own privacy policy, linked below.

2. Information the app does not collect

Sinema does not collect, request, transmit or store any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or any other contact details
  • Accounts, usernames or passwords — the app has no sign-in of any kind
  • Location data, whether precise or approximate
  • Contacts, calendar, photos, camera, microphone or files on your device
  • Advertising identifiers, and there is no advertising in the app
  • Installed-app lists
  • Payment or financial information — the app has no purchases

Usage and crash data is collected, and a device identifier is generated for that purpose. Section 4 describes it.

The app requests a single Android permission, INTERNET, which it needs to download film information and poster images. It requests no other permission.

3. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following locally so that it works offline and remembers your preferences. This information stays on your device. It is not uploaded, backed up to us, or shared.

WhatWhyHow to remove it
Cached film data — titles, overviews, ratings, genres and release dates So the app opens and works when you have no connection Settings → Clear cache, or uninstall
Cached poster and backdrop images So the app loads quickly and uses less data Settings → Clear cache, or uninstall
Your favourites So your saved films persist between sessions and offline Remove individually in the app, or uninstall
Your theme preference (system, light or dark) So the app looks the way you chose next time you open it Uninstall, or clear the app's storage in Android Settings

Uninstalling Sinema deletes all of it. You can also remove everything at any time from Android's Settings → Apps → Sinema → Storage → Clear storage.

4. Analytics, crash reporting and performance (Google Firebase)

The app uses three Google Firebase services: Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Performance Monitoring. All three are operated by Google, not by us, and what they send goes to Google.

What is sent

  • App activity — which screens you open, which film you opened and from where, and whether you followed a link out of the app. These are the events the app itself defines; it does not record what you search for or which films you favourite.
  • Crash data — if the app crashes, the technical details of the failure and a short trail of the events immediately before it.
  • Performance data — how long the app takes to start, how smoothly screens draw, and timing for the requests it makes to TMDB: the address requested, how long it took, how much data came back and whether it succeeded. The address is a TMDB endpoint, never a page you chose to visit, and no request body is recorded.
  • Device and identifiers — device model, operating system version, country, mobile carrier and connection type, and identifiers that Google generates for the installation. These identify a copy of the app, not a person.

What is not sent

No name, email address, account or contact detail, because the app never asks for one and has no sign-in. No location. No advertising identifier, and there is no advertising in the app.

Why

To find and fix faults on devices we do not own, and to see which parts of the app are used. Data is retained according to Google's policies, described in the Firebase privacy documentation and Google's Privacy Policy.

5. The Movie Database (TMDB)

All film information and imagery in Sinema comes from The Movie Database (TMDB), a third-party service. When you browse or search, the app requests data from api.themoviedb.org and images from image.tmdb.org.

Sinema sends TMDB only what is needed to answer the request — for example the search text you type, or the identifier of the film you opened. It does not send your name, your device identifiers or any profile information, because it does not have them. However, as with visiting any website, TMDB's servers will necessarily see your device's IP address along with standard request metadata. How TMDB handles that is governed by their policy:

  • TMDB Privacy Policy
  • TMDB Terms of Use

This product uses TMDB and the TMDB APIs but is not endorsed, certified, or otherwise approved by TMDB.

6. Google Play

If you installed Sinema from Google Play, Google may collect information about the installation and about crashes, independently of the app, under Google's Privacy Policy. Any crash or performance reports Google shows the developer are aggregated and do not identify you personally. This happens through the Play platform and your device settings, not through code in Sinema.

7. Children's privacy

Sinema is not directed at children under 13. The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us — although as described above, the app has no mechanism by which that could occur.

Note that film information comes from TMDB and may include titles and imagery intended for adult audiences.

8. Security

All network requests use encrypted HTTPS connections. Because the app stores nothing about you on any server, there is no account to be breached and no personal data of yours for us to lose. Information cached on your device is protected by Android's standard app sandboxing, which prevents other apps from reading it.

9. Your rights and choices

Because Sinema holds no personal data about you, there is no personal data for us to show, export, correct or delete on request. You remain in full control of everything the app stores locally: clear the cache from the app's Settings screen, remove favourites individually, or uninstall the app to erase all of it at once.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change — a new third-party service, or a new category of data sent to an existing one — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the "last updated" date at the top will change. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
